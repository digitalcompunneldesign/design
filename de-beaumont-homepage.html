05  Our method

From research to real-world impact

Evidence only counts when someone can act on it. Every project we fund travels the same four stages.

  1. Stage 01

    Research

    We field the questions no one else is asking — starting with the only nationally representative survey of the governmental public health workforce.

    PH WINS — fielded with ASTHO in 2014, 2017, 2021 and 2024.

  2. Stage 02

    Insights

    Findings are translated into plain language for the people who make decisions — not just for peer reviewers.

    Insights to Action — case studies and how-to guides vetted by the workforce itself.

  3. Stage 03

    Tools

    Playbooks, ratings, message testing and certificate programs — built so a two-person health department can use them on Monday.

    Practical Playbook · BEAM · PHRASES — training and implementation tools.

  4. Stage 04

    Action

    Cities adopt the policy. Departments change how they hire. Communities keep the gain long after the grant closes.

    CityHealth · BUILD — policy adoption and community-led change.
